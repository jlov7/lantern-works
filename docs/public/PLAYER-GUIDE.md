# Your first morning

Choose **First season: Main Street (guided)**. You have twelve days in this season,
and the morning waits while you decide what to do.

At the **Window**, select a neighbour. Read the request, price, deadline and capacity
forecast. Some ordinary prints let you change the words, palette and composition
before accepting. A larger commission starts with a conversation and a choice of
direction, followed by the drawing desk.

Accept work you can finish. The forecast accounts for today's commitments; its
redo warning tells you whether there is room for another attempt. You can turn
someone down or, when offered, arrange a later start. Review the other waiting
neighbours before opening the shop. Unanswered requests leave at day's end.

**Open the shop** starts production. **Work** shows your team making the accepted
pieces. You can pause, change playback speed or skip the animation. The evening
shows what was delivered and what happened to the promises you made. Review it,
then move to the next morning.

## Find your way around

| Place | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Window | Read requests, talk to neighbours, design work and make promises. |
| Work | Watch production and inspect the work on the benches. |
| Studio | Adjust the team and offers, plan a gathering, and look through your work. |
| Settings | Change speed, sound, motion and studio view, or reopen the guide. |

On a narrow screen, some actions move under **More**. The bottom navigation stays
with the workspace. Long pages scroll; you do not need to fit everything on screen
to make a decision.

## Make the print yours

Choose a layout, palette and lettering. Required customer words stay on the piece.
Illustrations can be moved with the pointer or keyboard controls. Inspect the
whole draft before approving the quote. Approval saves that exact composition;
delivery, the portfolio and later displays use the same work.

## Keep your season

The game attempts to save after each move in this browser. A save warning means
that recent progress may not survive closing the page. Private browsing, full
storage and browser cleanup can affect saves.

Select **Lantern Works** in the top bar (the lamp icon on narrow screens) to return
to the title and find **Your seasons**.
Use **Keep replay** beside a saved season, then **Copy replay link**. Keep that link somewhere you control before
clearing browser data or moving to another browser. It contains the starting
state and recorded moves, including original text you put on prints. Opening it
reconstructs the season without changing your saved histories. Choose **Save a copy
and continue** to keep it in this browser, or **Keep a copy** for a finished season.
Check that the copy saved before closing the tab. At the ending, you can also save
a postcard or copy a challenge for the same starting conditions.

A replay is a record of that season, not a backup of all settings and stamps.
An unusually large history may not fit into a replay link. Keep its browser data
until you have checked that the copied link opens successfully.

## Continue, or try another way

At a season's ending, you can revisit a morning and branch into a new history.
The original season is retained. A continuing studio instead carries selected
work, resources and relationships into its next chapter. The opening, practice
and legacy have an authored ending; open-studio play afterward is optional.

Fresh-start challenge scores are not applied to continuing studios, because
their starting resources differ.

## Controls and comfort

Tab moves between controls; Enter or Space activates a focused button. Escape
closes a dialog. Workspace tabs also support the arrow keys. When you are not
typing or inside a dialog, Space opens or pauses the workday, S skips its
playback, W opens the offer workshop, and Enter advances a completed evening.

Settings can follow the device's motion preference or reduce motion explicitly.
Sound is optional. Choose **Illustrated studio** if the miniature room is slow or
you prefer the painted view. Both show the same underlying work.

## Do I need AI?

No. The studio's AI production desk is simulated, just like its money and working
hours. It never calls an external AI service. The separate **Use Claude** option
can draft an offer or write a debrief with your own Anthropic key, but the complete
game and offline versions of those features work without it.
